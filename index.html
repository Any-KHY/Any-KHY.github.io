About Me

I am an analytical, detail-oriented professional based in Massey, Auckland, with a focus on data analysis and business analysis — building data pipelines, writing SQL, and translating complex findings into clear outputs that support real business decisions.

I recently completed a Master of Business Data Science with Distinction at the University of Otago. My thesis involved designing and evaluating a semi-automated brand-exposure detection pipeline, integrating computer vision and machine learning at scale. My IT and data analysis experience has been built through academic projects and coursework, and I am actively seeking my first professional role in this field.

My broader professional background spans over ten years in accounting and operations, primarily with firms in Hong Kong, with part-time experience in Japan and New Zealand. I speak Cantonese (native), Mandarin (fluent), and Japanese (JLPT Level 1), and am comfortable working in multicultural and regulated business environments.
